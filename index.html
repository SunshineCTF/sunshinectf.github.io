How to participate

  1. Register an account

    Head to the competition platform and register. Read the competition rules before playing.

  2. Get your team together

    Create a team or join your teammates on the platform. Each person may participate on only one registered team.

  3. Solve and submit

    When the event begins, open the challenge board. Solve challenges and submit flags through the competition platform to earn points.
